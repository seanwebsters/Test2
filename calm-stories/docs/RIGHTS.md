# Rights, voices and AI disclosure

## Creator content

Before an episode can be submitted for review, the creator completes a rights declaration for the series (`rights_declarations`, terms version `2026-01`):

- The story is their original work, or they hold the rights to it.
- Whether it was written with AI assistance.
- Any third-party material it includes, and on what basis.
- Consent for the selected narrator voice to be used.

Editors see the declaration next to the automated checks in the review queue. A missing declaration fails the rights check.

## Calm Originals

Series produced by Calm's own production engine are marked as Calm Originals everywhere they appear. Community series are labelled as creator originals. The production engine records a rights declaration for official series automatically and sets the series' AI disclosure.

## Voices

- Only stock voices from the voice provider's licensed library are offered. Each `voice_profiles` row records its provider voice id and the licence basis ("Licensed stock voice from the provider's library; no cloning").
- Voice cloning is not supported. There is no way to upload a voice sample.
- When no voice provider is connected, the simulated narrator is labelled as placeholder audio in Storymaker, the review queue and Creator Studio.

## AI disclosure

Series written with AI assistance show an "About this series" note on their public page. The AI disclosure check in editorial review warns when a series has none.

## Takedowns and reports

Listeners can report a series or episode from its page. Reports go to **Admin → Moderation**. Editors can dismiss a report, mark it actioned, or remove the series, which hides it everywhere and notifies its creator. Every moderation action is recorded in the audit trail.

## Sample content

The seeded catalogue's audio is procedurally synthesised ambience, and the seeded listening history is generated sample data. Both are labelled as such in the product.
